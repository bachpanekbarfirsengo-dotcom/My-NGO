🇮🇳 Official Government Helplines

Toll-Free Numbers — Completely Free to Call

These are official Government of India helplines. They are 100% toll-free — no charges on any network, 24 hours a day.

⭐ PRIMARY HELPLINE
14446

National De-Addiction Helpline

Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment
Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan

Direct counselling and guidance for drug de-addiction. Speak to a trained counsellor immediately for yourself or your family member.

✅ 100% Toll-Free ✅ 24/7 Available ✅ Hindi & English
Call 14446 — Free
NCB HELPLINE
1933

MANAS — NCB Narcotics Helpline

Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)
Government of India

Report drug trafficking, peddling, or illegal activities. Also provides information on counselling and rehabilitation centres near you.

✅ Toll-Free ✅ 24/7 Active ✅ Report & Get Help
Call 1933 — Free
MENTAL HEALTH
1800-599-0019

NIMHANS Mental Health Helpline

NIMHANS, Ministry of Health

Free mental health support including drug-related psychological issues. Expert psychiatrists and counsellors available round the clock.

⚠️ Know the Truth

Devastating Effects of Drugs

Drugs destroy lives, families, and futures. Understanding their harm is the first step toward prevention.

Prescription Pills Abuse
Tobacco & Nicotine
Alcohol Addiction
Hard Narcotics
❤️

Heart & Lung Failure

Drugs cause severe cardiac arrest, lung disease, and organ failure leading to premature death.

🧬

Brain Damage

Long-term drug use permanently alters brain chemistry, impairing memory, decision-making, and emotions.

🦷

Physical Deterioration

Rapid aging, tooth decay, skin lesions, severe weight loss and complete collapse of the immune system.

💉

Infectious Diseases

Needle sharing spreads HIV, Hepatitis B & C and other life-threatening blood-borne infections.

😰

Anxiety & Paranoia

Constant fear, panic attacks, and severe paranoia make daily life impossible for addicts.

😢

Depression

Drug-induced depression leads to hopelessness, suicidal thoughts, and complete loss of purpose.

🌀

Psychosis & Hallucinations

Losing touch with reality, violent behaviour, and seeing things that don't exist due to drug-induced psychosis.

🔒

Emotional Imprisonment

The inability to feel joy, love, or connection without drugs — trapped in a cycle of addiction.

👨‍👩‍👦

Family Breakdown

Addiction tears families apart — divorce, domestic violence, child neglect, and broken homes.

🚫

Social Isolation

Friends and loved ones distance themselves, leaving addicts completely alone and vulnerable.

⚖️

Criminal Activity

Desperation for drugs pushes people toward theft, violence, and other crimes, destroying their futures.

🏫

Ruined Education

Children of addicts drop out of school, miss opportunities, and carry trauma into adulthood.

💸

Financial Ruin

Addiction drains savings, forces debt, and leaves families in poverty with no way out.

🏢

Job Loss

Inability to maintain employment leads to permanent job loss and career destruction.

🏠

Homelessness

Millions of addicts lose their homes, living on streets with no shelter, food, or safety.

📉

National Productivity Loss

Drug addiction costs India billions annually in lost productivity, healthcare, and law enforcement.

👶 Children's Safety

Securing Our Children's Future

Children are our most precious resource. We work to ensure they grow up safe, educated, and free from the shadow of drugs.

50,000+ Children Educated About Drug Prevention
01

School Awareness Programs

We run drug awareness sessions in schools across India, teaching children to recognize, refuse, and report drug pressure from peers.

02

Parent & Teacher Training

Equipping parents and educators with tools to spot early warning signs of drug exposure and how to have open conversations.

03

Safe Spaces & Counselling

Creating safe, judgment-free spaces where children can speak openly and receive professional counselling support.

04

Scholarship & Skill Development

Providing scholarships and skill training to children from drug-affected families so poverty does not push them toward drugs.

💪 Recovery Journey

Our 5-Step Recovery Process

From the first call to full recovery — we walk every step with you.

📞

Step 1: Reach Out

Call 14446 (Govt toll-free) or WhatsApp us. No judgment, just support.

🩺

Step 2: Assessment

Trained counsellors assess the situation and create a personalised plan.

🏥

Step 3: Treatment

Connect with the right rehabilitation centre and begin recovery.

💬

Step 4: Counselling

Ongoing therapy, group sessions and mental health support.

🌟

Step 5: Reintegration

Job support, community integration, and long-term follow-up.
